PROJECT EXPLAINER

How HydroSim works

HydroSim is a simplified, fictional environment for demonstrating flood management workflows. It is not a hydrological forecast or a live GIS system.

01 / Set the conditions

Adjust river level, rainfall, drainage blockage, velocity and the sluice gate, or choose a preset.

02 / Observe impacts

The model estimates an effective stage and compares it with illustrative asset elevations and thresholds.

03 / Try interventions

Change pump capacity, deploy levees or clear drains to see how the simulated outcomes change.

04 / Review the response

Explore simulated road, rail and drainage statuses, actions and incident history. No real alerts are sent.
